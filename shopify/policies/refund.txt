Refund and cancellation policy

Changing or cancelling before the deadline

You can change or cancel your order, free of charge, at any time up to the
season deadline. A refund is returned to the card you paid with. A price difference on a changed order is
refunded or charged automatically.

After the deadline

Once orders close, the totals are final and the shipment is packed against them
in Eretz Yisrael. The store stops accepting changes at that point. A checkout
already in progress at the deadline may finish paying for up to 15 minutes
afterwards; nothing new can be started.

Past the deadline, get in touch and quote your order number. The contents are
settled.

If something is not right

Every item is inspected and approved by Morei Hora'ah in Eretz Yisrael before
the box is sealed, and reserve stock travels with the shipment. If something is
not right with what arrives, contact us as soon as it does, with a photograph
if something arrived damaged.

Tell us quickly rather than after Yom Tov. Arba Minim are perishable and the
season is short, so there is very little anyone can do once it has passed.

Returns

Arba Minim are perishable goods selected to order and are not returnable once
delivered.

Delivery charges

There is no shipping charge to refund: orders are collected in person, so
nothing is added on top of the price of the sets. A cancellation made before
the deadline returns what you paid, in full.
