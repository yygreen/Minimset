Refund and cancellation policy

Changing or cancelling before the deadline

You can change or cancel your order yourself, free of charge, at any time up to
the season deadline: Motzaei Shabbos, September 12, 8:30 PM EDT. A refund is
returned to the card you paid with. A price difference on a changed order is
refunded or charged automatically.

After the deadline

Once orders close, the totals are final and the shipment is packed against them
in Eretz Yisrael. The store stops accepting changes at that point. A checkout
already in progress at the deadline may finish paying for up to 15 minutes
afterwards; nothing new can be started.

Past the deadline, speak to your community rep. Their name and number are on
the pickup section of 4minimset.com.

The exchange guarantee

A Moreh Hora'ah will be present at distribution. If he determines an item is
not worth what you paid, it will be exchanged on the spot. Reserve stock is
flown in for exactly this, and the exchange happens there and then. You are
never asked to come back another day.

If you cannot collect

Anyone holding your order number can collect on your behalf, which is
deliberate: send someone else if you cannot come yourself. If nobody can come,
contact your community rep before the collection window closes.
