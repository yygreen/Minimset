Refund and cancellation policy

Changing or cancelling before the deadline

You can change or cancel your order, free of charge, at any time up to the
season deadline. A refund is returned to the card you paid with. A price difference on a changed order is
refunded or charged automatically.

After the deadline

Once orders close, the totals are final and the shipment is packed against them
in Eretz Yisrael. The store stops accepting changes at that point. A checkout
already in progress at the deadline may finish paying for up to 15 minutes
afterwards; nothing new can be started.

Past the deadline, get in touch and quote your order number. The contents are
settled, but a wrong delivery address can still be corrected until the box
leaves.

The replacement guarantee

If a Moreh Hora'ah would rule that what you received is not worth what you
paid, tell us and it is replaced from reserve stock at our cost. Reserve stock
is flown in for exactly this. You are not left arguing about it.

Damaged or missing on arrival

Tell us within two days of delivery, with a photograph if something arrived
broken. Arba Minim are perishable and the season is short, so a replacement
only helps if we hear quickly.

Returns

Arba Minim are perishable goods selected to order and are not returnable once
delivered. That is what the replacement guarantee above is for.

Shipping charges

The $7.99 shipping charge covers getting your order to you and is refunded
with a cancellation made before the deadline. On a package returned to us as
undeliverable, see the shipping policy.
