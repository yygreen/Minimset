Privacy policy

Who holds your information

V'samachta Arba Minim is a trading name of Stam Mehudar Co., a Delaware
corporation, of 123 Highgrove Crescent, Lakewood, NJ 08701, USA. That company
is responsible for the information described below.

What an order record holds

Only what is needed to fill and hand over that order:

- your name, phone number and email address
- the delivery address for the order
- what you ordered, the total, and how you paid
- your order number and the time it was placed
- the carrier and tracking number once it ships

Who sees it

Records are held in a private store that only the program can read. Staff
packing the season's shipment see the orders and their delivery addresses.
That is how a set reaches the right door.

Your address and name are also given to the carrier, so that they can deliver
the box.

Your email address

Used for your order confirmation and your tracking number. There is no mailing
list, and nothing is sold or passed to anyone outside the program.

Payment details

Card details are handled by Shopify's payment processor and are never stored by
the program.

How long records are kept

For the season and the reconciliation that follows it. To have your record
removed, get in touch.
