Privacy policy

What an order record holds

Only what is needed to fill and hand over that order:

- your name, phone number and email address
- your community, and your shul if you gave one
- what you ordered, the total, and how you paid
- your order number and the time it was placed

Who sees it

Records are held in a private store that only the program can read. Staff
running distribution for the season see the orders for their own community.
That is how a set gets handed to the right person on the day.

Your email address

Used for your order confirmation and your collection notice. There is no
mailing list, and nothing is sold or passed to anyone outside the program.

Payment details

Card details are handled by Shopify's payment processor and are never stored by
the program.

How long records are kept

For the season and the reconciliation that follows it. To have your record
removed, ask your community rep.
