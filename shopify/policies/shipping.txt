Shipping and collection policy

Nothing is posted or couriered.

Every set is collected in person at your community's host Beis Medrash on
distribution day, the day after Yom Kippur, between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM. You
choose your community at checkout, and your confirmation carries the exact
address and the rep's phone number for that location.

What arrives

The sets fly in from Eretz Yisrael sealed: the esrog in its box, the hadassim
and aravos in a sealed bag, the lulav sealed. You open them in your sukkah.

Collecting

Bring your order number. Distributors already know what you ordered and what
you paid, and a whole community is served in two to three hours. Anyone holding
your order number can collect for you.

If nobody collects on the day, contact your community rep. Sets are not held
indefinitely, and after Sukkos they are of no use to anyone.
