Shipping policy

Where we ship

Tracked shipping to any address in the United States, Alaska and Hawaii
included.

What it costs

$7.99 per order, however many sets are on it. Your own set and Chinuch sets for
the boys travel in the same box for the same charge. Carriage is typically 3 to
5 business days once your box leaves us.

When it arrives

Every set ships in time to arrive before Yom Tov. The season travels as one
shipment: it lands after Yom Kippur, and orders go out to the addresses on them
as it is unpacked. You get a tracking number by email the day your box leaves.

Delivery dates are the carrier's, not ours. Use an address where somebody can
take a box before Yom Tov, and watch the tracking as the days close in.

What arrives

The sets fly in from Eretz Yisrael sealed: the esrog in its box, the hadassim
and aravos in a sealed bag, the lulav sealed. You open them in your sukkah.

Wrong address

Check the address before you pay. You can correct it yourself from your order
page until the deadline, and after that by getting in touch, up until the box
leaves. A package returned to us as undeliverable can be sent again, but the
second shipping charge is yours and time is short.
