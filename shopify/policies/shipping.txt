Shipping and local pickup policy

Two ways to take your order

You choose at checkout: shipped to the address on your order, or collected in
person at our local pickup location. The price of the sets is the same either
way.

Where we ship

Tracked shipping to any address in the United States, Alaska and Hawaii
included.

What shipping costs

$7.99 per order, however many sets are on it. Your own set and Chinuch sets for
the boys travel in the same box for the same charge. Carriage is typically 3 to
5 business days once your box leaves us.

Local pickup

Free. Pickup is in Airmont, NY, and it is chosen at checkout in place of
shipping. There is no shipping charge on an order you collect.

The collection address and the window are on the notice you get when your order
is ready. Go by that notice, not by this page. Bring your order number.

If you cannot make the window, get in touch before it passes. An order left
uncollected is not refunded on that basis alone, but the season is short and we
would rather sort it out with you than after Yom Tov.

When it arrives

Every set is ready in time for Yom Tov. The season travels as one shipment: it
lands after Yom Kippur, orders to be shipped go out to the addresses on them as
it is unpacked, and orders to be collected are set aside at the pickup location.
You get a tracking number by email the day your box leaves, or a notice the day
your order is ready to collect.

Delivery dates are the carrier's, not ours. Use an address where somebody can
take a box before Yom Tov, and watch the tracking as the days close in.

What arrives

The sets fly in from Eretz Yisrael sealed: the esrog in its box, the hadassim
and aravos in a sealed bag, the lulav sealed. You open them in your sukkah.

Wrong address

Check the address before you pay. You can correct it by getting in touch, up
until the box leaves. A package returned to us as undeliverable can be sent
again, but the second shipping charge is yours and time is short.
