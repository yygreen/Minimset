Collection policy

No shipping this season

Orders are not posted or couriered. Every order is collected in person in
Airmont, NY. There is no shipping option and no shipping charge: the price of
the sets is the whole price.

Where and when

The collection address and the collection window are on the notice you get by
email the moment your order is ready. They come from us rather than from this
page, so go by that notice.

The whole season travels as one shipment. It lands after Yom Kippur, and your
order is set aside under your name as it is unpacked.

What to bring

Your order number. That is what identifies the order, so anyone can collect on
your behalf with it. You do not need to be the person who placed it.

If you cannot make the window

Get in touch before it passes rather than after. An order left uncollected is
not refunded on that basis alone, but the season is short and we would rather
sort it out with you than after Yom Tov.

What you collect

The sets fly in from Eretz Yisrael sealed: the esrog in its box, the hadassim
and aravos in a sealed bag, the lulav sealed. You open them in your sukkah.

If something is not right with what you collect, tell us there and then.
