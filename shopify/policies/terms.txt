Terms of service

Ordering

Orders are placed for the Sukkos 5787 season and are collected in person. You
choose your community, your sets and how many, and give a name, a phone number
and an email address so your confirmation can reach you.

Every order is given a number. That number is how you look your order up, how
you change it, and how it is handed to you at pickup. Anyone holding it can
collect on your behalf.

Payment

Sets are paid for in full at the time of ordering. The price you see is the
price charged. There is no shipping charge, because nothing is shipped.

The deadline

Ordering closes at the season deadline: Motzaei Shabbos, September 12,
8:30 PM EDT. The whole season travels as one shipment. When orders close the
totals are pulled, that exact quantity is packed in Eretz Yisrael, and it flies
in together with reserve stock for exchanges. Everything ordered by the
deadline is on that flight.

What you receive

A complete set at the level you chose: esrog, lulav, hadassim and aravos. Every
item is selected and inspected in Eretz Yisrael by Morei Hora'ah who are
experts in hilchos Daled Minim, then sealed.

Your order and payment make you a full partner in the Arba Minim being acquired
for your community.

Questions

Your community rep is the fastest route for anything about your own order.
Every rep is listed with a phone number on 4minimset.com.
