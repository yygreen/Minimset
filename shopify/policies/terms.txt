Terms of service

Who you are buying from

V'samachta Arba Minim is a trading name of Stam Mehudar Co., a Delaware
corporation, of 123 Highgrove Crescent, Lakewood, NJ 08701, USA. Your order is
a contract with that company.

Ordering

Orders are placed for the Sukkos 5787 season. You choose your sets and how
many, whether the order is shipped to you or collected at our local pickup
location, and you give a name, a phone number and an email address; a shipped
order also needs a delivery address.

Every order is given a number. That number is how you look your order up and
how you change it before the deadline.

Payment

Sets are paid for in full at the time of ordering, together with one flat
shipping charge of $7.99 per order, however many sets are on it. An order you
collect carries no shipping charge. The total is shown at checkout before you
pay.

The deadline

Ordering closes at the season deadline. The whole season travels as one
shipment. When orders close the
totals are pulled, that exact quantity is packed in Eretz Yisrael, and it flies
in together with reserve stock for replacements. Everything ordered by the
deadline is on that flight.

What you receive

A complete set at the level you chose: esrog, lulav, hadassim and aravos. Every
item is selected and inspected in Eretz Yisrael by Morei Hora'ah who are
experts in hilchos Daled Minim, then sealed.

Your order and payment make you a full partner in the Arba Minim being acquired
for the season.

Delivery and pickup

Every set is ready in time for Yom Tov. A shipped order goes tracked to the
address on it, typically 3 to 5 business days in carriage; delivery dates are
the carrier's. An order to be collected is held at the pickup location, and the
address and window are on the notice sent when it is ready. Shipping charges,
pickup, wrong addresses and undeliverable packages are covered in the shipping
and local pickup policy.
