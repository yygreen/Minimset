SHIPPING CONFIRMATION
Settings -> Notifications -> Shipping confirmation

The most-read email of the season, now that nobody is walking to a table for
it. Shopify's default already prints the tracking number and a Track button;
these lines go around it.

Subject
  Your Arba Minim are on the way

Opening

  Your set has left us.

  [ the tracking block the template already prints ]

Body

  Delivery dates are the carrier's, not ours. Watch the tracking as Yom Tov
  gets closer, and if the date looks wrong, tell us straight away rather than
  waiting for it to arrive.

  If nobody can take a box at that address, the carrier's own tracking page
  will let you redirect it or hold it for collection.

  Everything is sealed: the esrog in its box, the hadassim and aravos in a
  sealed bag, the lulav sealed. Open it in your sukkah.

  If what you received would not be worth what you paid, tell us. It is
  replaced from reserve stock at our cost -- reserve is flown in for exactly
  this.
