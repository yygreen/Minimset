READY FOR PICKUP
Settings -> Notifications -> Ready for pickup
The most-read email of the season. Everything a person needs to walk out the
door should be above the fold.

Subject
  Your Arba Minim are ready - collect today, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Opening

  Your set is ready.

  Collect it at [ the pickup location block the template already prints ],
  today, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

  Bring your order number.

Body

  If you cannot come yourself, send anyone with your order number. They can
  collect for you.

  A Moreh Hora'ah will be present. If he determines an item is not worth what
  you paid, it will be exchanged on the spot -- reserve stock is flown in for
  exactly this, and you are never asked to come back another day.

  Everything is sealed: the esrog in its box, the hadassim and aravos in a
  sealed bag, the lulav sealed. Open it in your sukkah.

  Questions today? Call your community rep, [ rep name and number ].
