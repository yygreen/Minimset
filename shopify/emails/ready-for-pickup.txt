READY FOR PICKUP
Settings -> Notifications -> Ready for pickup

This notification only goes to orders where the customer chose local pickup at
checkout. Shopify fills in the location's address and hours from the location
itself, which is why nothing here names them: keep the address and the window
correct on the Shopify location and this email stays correct on its own.

Subject
  Your Arba Minim are ready to collect

Opening paragraph (replace Shopify's default)

  Your order is here and set aside in your name. The address and the collection
  times are below.

After the pickup details, before the footer

  Bring your order number. Anyone can collect on your behalf with it.

  If you cannot make the times below, reply to this email before they pass
  rather than after. The season is short and we would rather sort it out with
  you now.

  Every item was selected and inspected in Eretz Yisrael by Morei Hora'ah who
  are experts in hilchos Daled Minim, then sealed: the esrog in its box, the
  hadassim and aravos in a sealed bag, the lulav sealed. It stays sealed until
  you open it in your sukkah.

  If something is not right with what you collect, tell us there and then.
