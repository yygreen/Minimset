The standard

What Kosher L'Bracha (Chinuch) means, word for word.

This is the sorting standard the Morei Hora'ah work to, exactly as the program writes it. Nothing summarized.

  • Esrog

    Clean of black dots as mentioned above, with more bletlech.

  • Lulav

    An open lulav, though certainly mostly closed.

  • Hadassim

    Mostly meshulash.

  • Aravos

    Fresh, sealed together with the hadassim in a securely sealed bag.

Questions about this set

What is in the box?

A complete set: esrog, lulav, hadassim and aravos. The esrog in its own box, the hadassim and aravos together in a sealed bag, the lulav sealed.

Who checked it?

Morei Hora'ah in Eretz Yisrael who are experts in hilchos Daled Minim. Every item is inspected and approved before it is sealed. A Moreh Hora'ah is also present at pickup.

What if I am not satisfied at pickup?

A Moreh Hora'ah will be present at distribution. If he determines an item is not worth what you paid, it will be exchanged on the spot.

Can I change my mind after ordering?

Yes, from your order page, any time before the deadline (Motzaei Shabbos, September 5, 8:30 PM EDT). The difference is refunded or charged automatically.
