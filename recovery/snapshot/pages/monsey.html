Questions

Asked in Monsey

Where do I pick up in Monsey?

At Wesley Hills Beis Medrash, Simcha Hall, Route 306, Monsey, NY 10952. Tuesday, September 22, 2026, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Bring your pickup code; anyone can collect for you with it.

Do I get to choose my esrog?

No, and that is the point. Morei Hora'ah in Eretz Yisrael sort and approve every item before it is sealed. What you open in your sukkah is what a Rav already chose. If the Motz at pickup rules an item is not worth what you paid, it is exchanged on the spot.

Can I order for the whole family in one go?

Yes. One order can hold a Mehudar A-A for you and Chinuch sets for the boys. Pay once, one code, one pickup.

Who do I call in Monsey?

R' Menachem Roth, (845) 555-0167. He is the local rep and runs the pickup table.
