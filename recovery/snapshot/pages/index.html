How it works

No table. No picking. No guessing.

In Eretz Yisrael this is simply how Arba Minim are bought. The picking is done for you, by Rabbanim, before the set ever leaves.

  1. 1

    Order and pay

    Pick your community and your sets. Pay in full before Motzaei Shabbos, September 5.

  2. 2

    The Rabbanim sort

    Morei Hora'ah in Eretz Yisrael select and inspect every item. Nothing is packed until it passes.

  3. 3

    It flies in sealed

    Esrog boxed, hadassim and aravos bagged, lulav sealed. One day in the air.

  4. 4

    You collect after Yom Kippur

    Show your code at your Beis Medrash. In and out in minutes, with a Moreh Hora'ah at the table.

The guarantee

The Motz is in the room.

A Moreh Hora'ah will be present at distribution. If he determines an item is not worth what you paid, it will be exchanged on the spot.

Reserve stock flies in for exactly this. Kasher v'yashar, one hundred percent, and you rely on the Rabbanim who bring it.

Questions

New concept in America. Old news in Yerushalayim.

Why can't I pick my own lulav?

Because a Rav already did. Every item is inspected and approved in Eretz Yisrael by Morei Hora'ah who are experts in hilchos Daled Minim, then sealed. You take it home and open it in your sukkah.

What if the Motz says it is not worth the money?

It is exchanged right there, on the spot, from reserve stock brought for exactly that. You are never told to come back another day.

What exactly is in a set?

All four: esrog, lulav, hadassim and aravos. The esrog in a box, the hadassim and aravos in a sealed bag, the lulav sealed. What changes between levels is how strictly each item was sorted.

Can I order for my children?

Yes. The Kosher L'Bracha (Chinuch) set is $40, so every boy holds his own minim. One order can hold a Mehudar A-A for you and Chinuch sets for the boys.

When and where do I pick up?

The day after Yom Kippur, at your community's host Beis Medrash, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Your confirmation has the address. Send anyone with your code if you cannot come.
